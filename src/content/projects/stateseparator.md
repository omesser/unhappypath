A Technion-era tool that takes an entangled quantum state and finds the closest
separable one. C++ and Eigen under a small web front end. After my old Prof and project guide [Yosi Avron](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yosi-avron-b9a28324/) told me it's **still useful in 2026** it undergone a revival, facelift, and public deployment (Jan 2026).
